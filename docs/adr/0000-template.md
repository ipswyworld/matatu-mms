# NNNN. Title — short, states the decision, not the problem

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Context

What forced this decision. The constraint, incident, or requirement that
made the status quo untenable — enough that someone reading this in a
year understands *why*, not just *what*.

## Decision

What was actually decided. One or two paragraphs, plain statement.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. This is the section that
saves the next person from re-litigating an option already ruled out.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, and what it leaves for
later. Include the honest downsides — an ADR that only lists upsides
isn't trustworthy.
